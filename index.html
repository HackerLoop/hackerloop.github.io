Tell us about your project

Interactive production for live audiences

We create live experiences that invite audiences to take part. With a background in gaming and esports, we combine storytelling, live content production and custom tools to connect brands with streamers and their communities.

On Twitch, participation can happen through chat, extensions, interactive overlays and channel points. We build the software behind these interactions around the creative concept and the streamer's editorial approach.

Creative technology, from screen to installation

Our Paris studio combines software development, hardware and electronics to turn creative concepts into interactive experiences. We work with agencies, publishers and brands on connected objects and interactive scenography, as well as tools for live broadcasts.

Whether your brief starts with a live campaign, a custom Twitch interaction or a physical installation, we connect the story with the technology that lets people participate.
